I always thought that “thinking in English” would be the best way to learn English. But every time I tried, I failed.

  

I live in South Korea, and I usually stay at home, so I don’t have many chances to speak or listen to English. To expose myself to an English environment, I try to think in English and watch videos in English.

  

But it’s really hard because I have a very strong alternative: Korean. It’s a thousand times easier to think and speak in Korean than in English.

  

Today, I happened to find a YouTube video titled “How to Think in English — Improve Your English.” I was very curious: “Is there some secret sauce I don’t know about?”

  

It wasn’t very practical, but it reminded me that my original idea was reasonable.

  

I’ve been learning English for 23 years. That’s a very long time. I’m surprised that even after more than 20 years, I still haven’t mastered it — or even become truly good at it.

  

Reading and listening are fine. Speaking and writing are hard, and thinking in English feels almost impossible.

---

“But when it comes to forming your own thoughts, it suddenly feels like a big wall stands in your way.”

Method 1: Train your inner voice

“If possible, try to create situations where English is the only option.”
