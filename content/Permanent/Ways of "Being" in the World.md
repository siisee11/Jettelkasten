### East Asian Cultures
* Underestimate our ability
* Focus on tasks that we do NOT do well
* Being self critical will enhance our reputation
* Self improvement will lead to a successful life

[[Western]]
### Western Cultures
* Overestimate our abilities
* Focus on what we do well
* Being more talented than others improves our reputation
* High self esteem will lead to a successful life
